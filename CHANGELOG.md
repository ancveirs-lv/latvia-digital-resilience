# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-22

Pilot-ready resilience and assurance model:

- four bounded `pilot_ready` modules:
  - public-sector ICT lifecycle traceability;
  - CVD authorization and evidence;
  - defensible vulnerability prioritisation;
  - degraded operations and recovery;
- 20 registered sources and 21 claim-level evidence records;
- machine-readable proposal, control and pilot registries:
  - 4 proposals;
  - 16 verifiable controls;
  - 4 bounded pilots with prerequisites and predeclared acceptance evidence;
- 40 proposal-specific skeptical-review findings across ten fixed review lenses;
- bilingual ten-lens skeptical review and compliance/authority boundary crosswalk;
- strengthened JSON Schemas and graph integrity validation for proposals, controls, pilots and reviews;
- 17 bilingual document pairs / 34 localized pages;
- expanded source-backed evidence for VIRSIS ICT coordination, CVSS v4.0, EPSS, CISA KEV and NIS2 continuity;
- explicit safeguards that project controls are non-normative, CVD does not extend third-party authority, vulnerability signals are not collapsed into a universal score, and the degraded-operations state model remains project-defined.
