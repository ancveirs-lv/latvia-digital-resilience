# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve evidence quality, technical precision, implementation feasibility, or bilingual clarity.

A strong issue or pull request contains:

1. the problem or claim being addressed;
2. the source-backed baseline;
3. the proposed change;
4. scope and non-goals;
5. risks or trade-offs;
6. measurable validation criteria;
7. matching Latvian and English changes when public documentation is affected.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. Latvian legislation and official institutional material;
2. EUR-Lex and EU institutional material;
3. CERT.LV, ENISA, FIRST, IETF, NIST and comparable technical authorities;
4. standards bodies and peer-reviewed research;
5. secondary analysis only when stronger primary material is unavailable.

Do not convert a recommendation into a factual or legal claim merely by attaching a citation.

## Do not submit

- exploitation instructions against a live system;
- credentials, secrets or restricted configuration;
- personal data;
- unsupported allegations about identifiable people or organisations;
- party-political campaigning;
- generated text that has not been source-checked and edited;
- material that silently mixes legal requirements with voluntary good practice.

Run `make check` before opening a pull request.
