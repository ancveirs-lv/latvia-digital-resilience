# Compliance and authority boundaries

This is a boundary map, not a legal opinion.

| Module | Source-backed baseline | Project-defined layer | Must not be implied |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICT lifecycle traceability | VIRSIS scope and VARAM transition of ICT activity coordination | Minimal evidence pointer and lifecycle mapping | Law requires this exact schema or a new registry |
| CVD authorization | National Cybersecurity Law, CERT.LV programme terms, RFC 9116 | Programme quality controls and pilot criteria | Universal test permission, pentest equivalence or third-party authority |
| Vulnerability prioritisation | FIRST CVSS/EPSS, CISA KEV, NIS2/CRA context | Explainable multi-signal decision record | One universal risk score or replacement of duties |
| Degraded operations | NIS2 continuity and EU election-cybersecurity guidance | NORMAL → DEGRADED → OFFLINE/MANUAL → RECOVERY/RECONCILIATION | Official Latvian taxonomy or disclosure of sensitive recovery detail |

A project control can be useful without being legally mandatory. Before institutional adoption, the implementing body must determine applicable law, sector rules, contracts, data classifications and authority boundaries.
