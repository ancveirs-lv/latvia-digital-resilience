# CVD authorization and evidence framework

CVD, permission to test, Bug Bounty and penetration testing are related but not interchangeable. Latvia's National Cybersecurity Law contains CVD provisions; CERT.LV operates the reporting platform and programme terms can define resources and conditions. RFC 9116 says `security.txt` does not itself imply permission to test.

## Project proposal

A public-sector programme should publish four boundaries: **assets**, **conduct**, **authority**, and **evidence flow**. Safe-harbour language, where used, must remain within authority the resource owner can actually grant and must not imply authority over third-party systems.

## Pilot

Use a small set of internet-facing resources with known ownership. Reconcile scope against the asset inventory and third-party dependencies before publication.
