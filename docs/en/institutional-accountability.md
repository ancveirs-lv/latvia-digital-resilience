# Institutional accountability

## Source-backed boundary

Latvian whistleblowing law protects reporting about wrongdoing that may harm the public interest and distinguishes it from reporting solely about a personal-interest infringement.

That legal distinction should not be used to dismiss legitimate grievances; it means the processes have different purposes and safeguards.

## Project recommendation

Institutions should keep separate but interoperable paths for:

- whistleblowing and protected public-interest reports;
- individual grievance or complaint handling;
- incident and security reporting;
- audit findings;
- improvement proposals;
- correction of public factual errors.

For systemic issues, record the allegation or observation, evidence state, responsible owner, decision, corrective action, validation result, and closure rationale.

This repository is not a forum for publishing unsupported allegations about identifiable people or organisations.
