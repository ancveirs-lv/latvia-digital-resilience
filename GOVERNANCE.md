# Governance and editorial policy

This is a maintainer-led public reference. Changes are accepted on evidence and implementation quality, not on institutional, commercial, or political affiliation.

## Decision rules

- Source-backed facts must remain distinguishable from project recommendations.
- Legal claims must identify the applicable jurisdiction and source.
- A source supports only the proposition actually established by that source.
- Negative findings are bounded to the reviewed source set.
- Election-related material is technical and organisational; the project does not recommend electoral choices.
- Identifiable people or organisations are not labelled with allegations without documented facts and an appropriate public-interest basis.
- Sensitive operational detail is excluded from public documentation.

## Change classes

- **Evidence correction:** source, locator, factual or legal boundary.
- **Editorial correction:** clarity, translation, accessibility, metadata.
- **Proposal change:** a new or materially changed recommendation.
- **Infrastructure change:** build, schema, CI, deployment, security.

Material evidence and proposal changes should be reviewed through a pull request and must pass the automated quality gate.
